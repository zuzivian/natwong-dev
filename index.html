ABOUT

My career started in a role where the cost of a bad system was measured in the worst possible terms — commanding an air defense unit means the feedback loop between decision and consequence is immediate and unforgiving. That's the lens I've carried into everything since: engineering degrees that taught me how systems are built, and now a Stanford MBA aimed at learning how to decide what gets built at all.

I'm drawn to problems that sit at intersections — hardware and software, human judgment and automated systems, operational discipline and product ambition. I'm currently exploring product management in tech, with a particular interest in the infrastructure, ML tooling, and platform layers where technical depth and product judgment both matter.
